## 🚀 The Flagship: Sarkar-AGI Core 1

Our primary focus is the development, scaling, and alignment of **Sarkar-AGI Core 1**—our next-generation neural-cognitive engine designed to bridge the gap between specialized AI and true cross-domain reasoning. 

### Key Capabilities of Core 1:
* **Continuous Learning:** Adapts to new environments and tasks without catastrophic forgetting.
* **Unified Multi-Modal Reasoning:** Native processing across text, code, vision, and sensory data streams.
* **Autonomous Decision Trees:** Advanced long-horizon planning and self-correction protocols.

## 🧠 Focus Areas

* **AGI Architecture:** Iterating on the Core 1 engine to achieve stable, human-level generalized intelligence.
* **Autonomous Agents:** Building production-ready agentic networks powered by the Core 1 substrate.
* **Safety & Alignment:** Developing mathematical and behavioral safeguards directly into the core training loops.
